Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your tasks and notes

GlassDo keeps your tasks, projects, tags, and notes entirely on your device (local storage). This content is not sent to any server today.

Cross-device sync is planned as an opt-in feature; once it ships, it will only run when you explicitly turn it on and it will be tied to your account. This policy will be updated once sync launches.

Your system monitoring data

CPU, memory, disk, battery, and network measurements are processed and displayed only on your device. This data is never sent to the cloud.

Anonymous usage analytics

We ask for permission on first launch. If you accept, the following anonymous data — never including your personal content — is recorded: a locally generated device identifier that doesn't identify you, device model, OS and app version, country/language (from system settings only — no IP address is used), and daily launch count.

You can turn this permission off anytime from Settings. Analytics never affects the app's core functionality.

What we don't collect

Your IP address, name, email address, or location (GPS) are never collected, under any circumstances.

For any questions, check out our support page.
