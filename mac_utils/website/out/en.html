Three things, done right

Stay Focused

Your tasks wait at the edge of the screen; open and close them without ever leaving your main app.

See Your Mac

CPU, memory, disk, battery, and network — your whole system at a glance, with live graphs.

Capture Anything, Fast

Add whatever crosses your mind — a task or a note — in seconds, from the panel or the menu bar.

Edge Rail: a panel that lives on the edge of your screen

Rest it against any edge of your screen, open it on hover. Switch between the rail and the expanded view, tuck it away (sliver) or pin it in place — without the panel frame ever shifting.

  • Can rest against any screen edge
  • Sliver / peek and pinned modes
  • The frame stays put while you switch icons

Tasks and notes, in one place

Group by project or tag, add priority and due dates, keep multi-line notes. Smart lists — Today, Upcoming, All, Completed — help you find what you need instantly.

  • Grouping by project or tag
  • Smart lists and search
  • Notes you can pop out of the panel into free-floating windows

Watch your system in real time

CPU (per core), memory, disk, battery, and network speed — with live cards and graphs. Network speed tests and disk space analysis too.

This data never leaves your device.

Widgets and the menu bar

Today's tasks and a system summary in your desktop and Notification Center widgets. In the menu bar, whichever meter you want, in whichever format: bar, percentage, bytes, or graph.

Sync, on your terms

Sync, on your terms

Your tasks and notes can optionally sync across your devices. Your system monitoring data (CPU, RAM, disk, network) never goes to the cloud by default — it stays on your own Mac.

  • Task/note sync is opt-in and scoped to your account
  • System metrics are never uploaded
  • Anonymous usage analytics only contribute if you allow it
